# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a friendly place to learn and teach.

## Our standards

- Be kind and respectful. Assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- Welcome beginners. No question is too basic.
- Credit other people's work and only submit content you have the right to share.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, spam, and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions, and may block people who break these rules. To report a problem, open a private security advisory or contact the maintainer listed on the repository profile.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
