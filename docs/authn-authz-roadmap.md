# Authentication & Authorization (AuthN/AuthZ) Roadmap

[Explore the live roadmap](https://samikhan43.github.io/roadmap-hub/Auth-roadmap/)

This production-live learning path takes you from identity and access-management fundamentals to senior-level application security practice. Across **6 levels and 26 topics**, it covers authentication and authorization concepts including passwords and sessions, OAuth and OpenID Connect (OIDC), multi-factor authentication (MFA), authorization policies, federation, and multi-tenant IAM.

The roadmap includes interactive lessons, practical challenges, level projects, and progress tracking saved in your browser. It is designed to help learners understand how to build and evaluate secure identity flows and access controls in real-world applications.
